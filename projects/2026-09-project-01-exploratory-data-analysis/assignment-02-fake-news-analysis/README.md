# Assignment 02 - Fake News Data Analysis

## Objective

The purpose of this assignment is to explore and compare Fake and True news datasets using Python.

The focus is on data cleaning, preprocessing and exploratory data analysis.

You are not required to build a fake-news classification model at this stage.

## Datasets

Two datasets are required:

- `Fake (1).csv`
- `True.csv`

The datasets were distributed privately by the mentor and are not stored in this GitHub repository.

## Assignment Tasks

### 1. Load and Inspect the Data

Load both datasets and examine:

- number of rows and columns;
- column names;
- data types;
- missing values;
- duplicate records; and
- the overall structure of the data.

### 2. Prepare the Data

Create a way of identifying whether an article came from the Fake or True dataset.

Combine the datasets where appropriate for comparative analysis.

Document your approach clearly.

### 3. Clean and Preprocess the Data

Investigate and address relevant data-quality problems.

Consider:

- missing or blank text;
- duplicate articles;
- inconsistent dates;
- text formatting;
- unusual records; and
- any other issues you discover.

Explain important cleaning decisions.

### 4. Exploratory Data Analysis

Compare Fake and True news articles.

Your analysis may investigate:

- number of Fake and True articles;
- article subjects or categories;
- publication dates;
- article-title length;
- article-text length;
- word counts;
- patterns over time; and
- other meaningful differences between Fake and True news.

### 5. Text Exploration

Where appropriate, explore the language used within the articles.

This may include examining frequently occurring words or other simple text characteristics.

Avoid drawing conclusions that are not supported by the data.

### 6. Visualisation

Create appropriate visualisations to communicate important findings.

Each visualisation should include a clear explanation of what it shows.

### 7. Findings

Identify the most important patterns and differences discovered during your analysis.

The objective is to understand the dataset before any future modelling work.

## Expected Submission

Your submission should contain:

- `fake_news_analysis.ipynb`
- `report.md` or a PDF report

## Suggested Report Structure

### 1. Introduction

Explain the purpose of the analysis.

### 2. Dataset Overview

### 3. Data Cleaning and Preparation

### 4. Key Findings

Present the most important insights and comparisons.

### 5. Conclusion

Summarise what the exploratory analysis reveals about the datasets.
